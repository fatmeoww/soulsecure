--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Flag 4 (harder mode) -- combined/deep scan, binary-embedded secret
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target:  soulsecure-webapp-dump/assets/logo.png -- a structurally valid PNG with
         a Stripe-shaped secret (STRIPE_API_KEY=sk_live_...) appended AFTER the
         IEND chunk (image viewers ignore trailing data; deep/combined scanners
         don't).

Run:
    gitleaks detect --source ./soulsecure-webapp-dump --no-git -v
    # or, to see it directly:
    tail -c 200 soulsecure-webapp-dump/assets/logo.png

NOTE: gitleaks scans this file's raw bytes regardless of extension and WILL
surface it. trufflehog's `filesystem` scanner, by contrast, recognizes this
file as a PNG image by content and skips it entirely by design (0 chunks
scanned) -- this holds even with --no-force-skip-binaries and even if you
rename the file's extension, since trufflehog sniffs content, not the name.
That's a genuine, useful lesson: different scanners make different binary-
handling trade-offs, and "the tool didn't flag it" is not the same as "there's
nothing there." Use gitleaks for this one.

Finding it (however you found it) yields Flag 4:

    flag{25ee7fa4ece475253ec07dd7d82e8c2d}
